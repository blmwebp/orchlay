## How content was adapted

The supplied Orchlay Word document is the authoritative source for the text and service categories. The supplied Techtonica presentation is supporting context, not evidence of Orchlay client relationships.

Sections in the React page:

1. Hero: Microsoft Technology. Engineered for Business.
2. Five core capabilities, with expandable details and business outcomes.
3. Acterys planning and analytics introduction with five deliverables.
4. International delivery experience: United States and Australia.
5. About Orchlay, delivery approach, and distinguishing strengths.
6. Contact form with the fields specified in the Word document.

Techtonica's named testimonials, client logos, statistics, people, and actual images were not copied or attributed to Orchlay. The Orchlay brand mark is an original temporary wordmark, not a claim to be Orchlay's official identity. Replace it with the official Orchlay logo before launch if one is available.

The typography and colors are deliberately close to the reference; spacing and mobile layouts are updated for better accessibility and a content-complete single-page site.
